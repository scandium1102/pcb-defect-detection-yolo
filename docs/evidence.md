# Evidence scope

## Included

The public figures in `assets/` show recorded course-project evaluation outputs:

- five-class and nine-class training summaries;
- confusion matrices and normalized confusion matrices;
- precision-recall curves;
- representative model predictions.

## Excluded

- Original PCB images and annotation files.
- Weights, raw logs, notebook checkpoints, and local runtime paths.
- Any claim of industrial deployment, inspection yield, or production accuracy.

The reported mAP@0.5 values are historical results from this educational experiment, not a current benchmark or an independently reproduced result.
